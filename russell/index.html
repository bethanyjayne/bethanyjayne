A bear, a pirate ship and a very big adventure.

Character profile

Russell

Handmade picture book · Printed and ring-bound

Russell is a very simple bear, whose main joys in life are long naps and a good snack. His quiet morning in his cosy cave is interrupted when he discovers a mysterious pirate ship in the clouds, and he is swept away on a magical journey full of surprises, strange places and a very bossy cat captain.
